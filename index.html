Dr. Solomon Chukwuemeka Awusah (Non-Clinical Strategist)

Dr. Solomon Awusah is a non-clinical strategist and executive who leads Vesters Edge Real Estate Development and Management Company and EPM Logistics Consulting.

About

Dr. Awusah brings a background in business operations, strategy and leadership to the companies he runs. As President of Vesters Edge, he oversees real estate development and property management. As CEO of EPM Logistics Consulting, he advises organizations on logistics and operations.

He earned his undergraduate degree at Michigan State University and his graduate degree at Wayne State University, and is based in the United States.
