Dr. Solomon Chukwuemeka Awusah, MD

Dr. Solomon Awusah is an internist at Maecenas Health Systems in Southfield, Michigan, providing primary care for adults.

About Dr. Awusah

Dr. Solomon C. Awusah is an internal medicine physician who graduated from Wayne State University School of Medicine in 2008. His practice covers general internal medicine and geriatric medicine, including check-ups, immunizations, weight management, heartburn and high blood pressure care.

Dr. Awusah sees patients at Maecenas Health Systems PC in Southfield and holds an active Michigan medical license.
