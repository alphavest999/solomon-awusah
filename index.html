Solomon Chukwuemeka Awusah

Solomon Awusah is an executive and project manager who leads Vesters Edge Real Estate Development and Management Company and EPM Logistics Consulting.

About

Solomon C. Awusah brings a background in business operations, strategy and leadership to the companies he runs. As President of Vesters Edge, he oversees real estate development and property management. As CEO of EPM Logistics Consulting, he advises organizations on logistics and operations.

He is a graduate of Wayne State University and is based in the United States.
